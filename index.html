CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM

The Four Waste Streams

Segregating at the source prevents cross-contamination and maximizes landfill diversion.

Blue Bin

Recyclable

Materials that can be reprocessed into fresh goods, conserving virgin raw resources and energy.

Common items:
Plastic Bottles Aluminum Cans Cardboard Glass Jars Magazines
Tip: Must be empty, rinsed & dry before binning.
Green Bin

Organic

Biodegradable plant and food waste suited for industrial composting or home vermiculture.

Common items:
Fruit Peels Coffee Grounds Eggshells Garden Leaves Food Leftovers
Tip: Never wrap organic waste in non-compostable plastic bags.
Special / Red Bin

Hazardous

Toxic, corrosive, or flammable materials posing severe threats to human health and groundwater.

Common items:
Lithium Batteries Paint & Thinner Light Bulbs Pesticides Electronics (E-Waste)
Tip: Take to certified municipal e-waste drops. Never put in general trash.
Black / Grey Bin

General Waste

Residual non-recyclable, non-compostable trash destined for waste-to-energy incineration or landfill.

Common items:
Plastic Cutlery Chip Packets Greasy Wax Paper Sanitary Items Broken Ceramics
Tip: Aim to reduce single-use multi-layer packaging whenever possible.
